Common questions

Where is my money held?
In your own account at Interactive Brokers. If you already have one, we link it to my firm. If not, you open one and I help with the paperwork. I can place trades and deduct the advisory fee you’ve authorized. I can’t withdraw or transfer your assets, and you can log in and see every position and trade.
How are you paid?
1% of assets per year, billed quarterly in arrears and deducted from your account. I don’t receive commissions or any payments from fund companies. Fund expense ratios and IBKR’s trading costs are separate. Full terms are in Form ADV Part 2A.
How do you invest?
For most taxable accounts I use a model I call Core & Overlay. The core is U.S. stocks and gold, held for years and rarely sold. The overlay is a set of exchange-traded futures on stocks, Treasuries and other markets, turned up or down by fixed rules. Because I manage risk through the futures, I can cut exposure without selling the core and triggering capital gains. The overlay uses leverage, which makes losses bigger as well as gains. If that isn’t a fit for you, I’ll say so.
How often will we talk?
I review each account every quarter and send a written report. I rebalance when a holding drifts outside its target range. Between reviews, email or call me whenever something changes.
Do you work with clients outside New York?
Sometimes. I can only work with clients in states where I’m registered or exempt from registration. Tell me where you live and I’ll check.
How do we get started?
Email me a few lines about your situation. We’ll set up a call to see whether I can help. If we go ahead, I’ll handle the paperwork, whether that means linking your existing IBKR account or opening a new one.
