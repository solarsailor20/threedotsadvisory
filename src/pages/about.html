About

Yuheng (Alan) Fu, CFA

Founder & Portfolio Manager

Alan is the founder and portfolio manager of Three Dots Advisory, a registered investment adviser based in Murray Hill, Manhattan.

He started out as a data scientist at Microsoft, building quantitative models, and managed money for family and friends on the side. He has been investing since 2014.

In 2019 he founded Three Dots Capital, the predecessor to Three Dots Advisory, where he invested in blockchain infrastructure and ran systematic trading strategies for clients.

Today he manages client money with the same rules-based methods. Many of his clients are professionals with equity compensation, and many have ties to mainland China and Hong Kong. He works in English and Mandarin.

Alan holds a BS in Computer Science, with a minor in Economics, from Northwestern University.
